# ⚛️ Kuantizma

> Explore the fascinating and mysterious subatomic universe. Discover quantum mechanics, entanglement, superposition, and more through an interactive, visually stunning interface.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [kuantizma.tiiny.site](https://kuantizma.tiiny.site)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Glassmorphic Login Portal:** A sleek, premium entrance featuring custom CSS floating light-burst animations and seamless client-side authentication.
- **Interactive Concept Cards:** Dive deep into 10 foundational quantum physics concepts with custom interactive cards that launch detailed info modals.
- **Branches of Quantum Study:** A categorized interactive checklist exploring advanced fields, from Quantum Computing to Quantum Biology.
- **Fully Responsive & Fluid UI:** Fully optimized design featuring fluid typography and adaptive grids that look premium on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- **Futuristic Dark Theme:** Styled with deep hues, clean radial gradients, and neon cyan accents reminiscent of laboratory instruments.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Structure:** HTML5 (Semantic elements)
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS3 (Custom design system using CSS variables, Flexbox, CSS Grid, custom keyframe animations, and glassmorphism)
- **Logic:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ state management, interactive modal logic, and `sessionStorage` session caching)

## 📖 Quantum Topics Covered

Clicking any card in the application reveals a detailed breakdown of:
1. **Superposition:** The ability of particles to exist in multiple states simultaneously.
2. **Quantum Entanglement:** Einstein's "spooky action at a distance."
3. **Double-Slit Experiment:** The benchmark experiment for wave-particle duality.
4. **Quantum Tunneling:** Particles passing through classically insurmountable energy barriers.
5. **Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle:** The fundamental physics limit on position and momentum measurement.
6. **Wave Function:** The mathematical state of a quantum system.
7. **Quantum Spin:** Intrinsic angular momentum of subatomic particles.
8. **Decoherence:** The transition of quantum systems into classical states due to environmental interaction.
9. **Planck's Constant:** The fundamental constant defining energy quanta.
10. **Schrödinger's Cat:** The famous thought experiment highlighting macro-superposition.
